Best AI for 3D assets

Generate props, characters and models from text or images.

Last checked: Oct 22, 2026 · Published by Lali Publications. Picks researched by our AI editor and checked against each tool's official site. How we pick

Quick answer: Meshy. Top pick: solid text/image-to-3D with textures, and exports for game engines.

Our picks

  1. 1. Meshy

    Text/image-to-3D model generation for game assets.

    Verdict: Top pick: solid text/image-to-3D with textures, and exports for game engines.

    Price: Free plan: 100 credits a month. Pro $20/mo.

  2. 2. Tripo

    Fast text/image-to-3D generator with auto-rigging and clean topology options.

    Verdict: Fast generation with auto-rigging; great for characters you need to animate.

    Price: Free plan. Pro $20/mo billed yearly; Max $90/mo billed yearly.

  3. 3. Hyper3D Rodin

    Text/image-to-3D generator with clean topology, PBR materials and plugins for Blender, Unity and Unreal.

    Verdict: Detailed text- or image-to-3D with clean topology; plugins drop models straight into Blender, Unity or Unreal.

    Price: Free to generate; you pay $1.50 per credit only when you keep a result. Subscription $30/mo ($24/mo billed yearly).

  4. 4. Scenario

    Generates style-consistent game art assets from your own training sets.

    Verdict: Best for style-consistent game assets across a whole project.

    Price: Free plan outputs are for personal and evaluation use only. Paid plans from $15/mo (Starter).

  5. 5. Stable Diffusion

    Open image model family you can run locally (e.g. via ComfyUI).

    Verdict: Skip 3D entirely for 2.5D/sprite games — generate concept art and textures locally.

Prices checked Oct 22, 2026 and Oct 23, 2026 on each tool's official pricing page. They change often, so the official page wins.

Picks are editorial and not paid. Pricing and features change often — check each official site.
