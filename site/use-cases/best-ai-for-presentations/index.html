Our picks

  1. 1. Gamma

    Generates presentations, docs and web pages from a prompt or outline.

    Verdict: Top pick: prompt or paste notes and get a good-looking deck in a minute; exports to PowerPoint.

  2. 2. Beautiful.ai

    Slide software with smart templates that auto-format as you add content.

    Verdict: Best for teams who want consistent on-brand slides that stay formatted.

  3. 3. Claude

    Anthropic's AI assistant, strong at long documents, writing and code.

    Verdict: Write the story and outline first, or have it generate slide content and code-based decks.

  4. 4. ChatGPT

    OpenAI's general-purpose AI assistant with chat, images, voice and search.

    Verdict: Good for outlines and speaker notes, and it can produce a downloadable .pptx file.

  5. 5. Microsoft 365 Copilot (PowerPoint)

    Copilot inside PowerPoint: drafts a deck from a prompt or file, adds slides and rewrites or reorganizes content.

    Verdict: Makes sense if your decks already live in PowerPoint and your Microsoft 365 plan includes Copilot.

Picks are editorial and not paid. Pricing and features change often — check each official site.
