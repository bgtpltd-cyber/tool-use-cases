Our picks

  1. 1. Perplexity

    AI answer engine that searches the web and cites sources.

    Verdict: Fast cited answers from the live web; the go-to for quick research.

  2. 2. NotebookLM

    Google's research notebook grounded in your own uploaded sources.

    Verdict: Upload your own sources and get grounded answers (and audio overviews).

  3. 3. ChatGPT

    OpenAI's general-purpose AI assistant with chat, images, voice and search.

    Verdict: Deep research mode produces long, cited reports on a topic.

  4. 4. Gemini

    Google's AI assistant with huge context windows and Google integration.

    Verdict: Deep Research plus Google Search integration.

  5. 5. Elicit

    AI research assistant for finding and summarizing academic papers.

    Verdict: Purpose-built for academic papers and literature reviews.

  6. 6. Grok

    xAI's assistant with real-time X/web search.

    Verdict: Useful for what people are saying right now on X.

Picks are editorial and not paid. Pricing and features change often — check each official site.
