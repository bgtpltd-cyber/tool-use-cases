Best AI for sales emails

Write better cold and follow-up emails — personal, short, and not obviously AI.

Last checked: Oct 22, 2026 · Published by Lali Publications. Picks researched by our AI editor and checked against each tool's official site. How we pick

Quick answer: Claude. Top pick: give it the prospect's context and get a short, human-sounding draft. Always edit.

Our picks

  1. 1. Claude

    Anthropic's AI assistant, strong at long documents, writing and code.

    Verdict: Top pick: give it the prospect's context and get a short, human-sounding draft. Always edit.

    Price: Free plan. Pro $20/mo ($17/mo billed yearly); Max from $100/mo.

  2. 2. ChatGPT

    OpenAI's general-purpose AI assistant with chat, images, voice and search.

    Verdict: Equally good for drafts and variants; use custom instructions to set your voice.

    Price: Free plan. Paid plans: Go, Plus ($20/mo) and Pro.

  3. 3. Lavender

    Sales email coach that scores and rewrites cold emails inside your inbox.

    Verdict: Coaching and scoring right in Gmail/Outlook; worth it if you send cold email daily.

  4. 4. Perplexity

    AI answer engine that searches the web and cites sources.

    Verdict: Quick prospect and company research before you write the first line.

  5. 5. Grammarly

    Writing assistant for grammar, tone and rewrites everywhere you type.

    Verdict: Final polish on tone and clarity everywhere you type.

Prices checked Oct 22, 2026 on each tool's official pricing page. They change often, so the official page wins.
