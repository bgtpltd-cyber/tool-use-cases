Our picks

  1. 1. Descript

    Edit video and podcasts by editing the transcript.

    Verdict: Edit talking-head videos like a doc; removes filler words. Huge time saver for YouTubers.

  2. 2. Runway

    AI video generation and editing suite (Gen-series models).

    Verdict: Pro-grade AI video generation and editing tools for b-roll and effects.

  3. 3. Google Veo

    Google DeepMind's text-to-video model with native audio.

    Verdict: High-quality generated clips with sound; strong for cinematic b-roll.

  4. 4. ElevenLabs

    Realistic AI voiceover, voice cloning and dubbing.

    Verdict: Best-in-class voiceover and dubbing into other languages.

  5. 5. OpusClip

    Turns long videos into short vertical clips automatically.

    Verdict: Turns a long video or stream into Shorts/TikToks automatically.

  6. 6. CapCut

    Free-tier video editor with auto-captions and AI effects.

    Verdict: Free-ish editor with solid auto-captions — the practical everyday tool.

  7. 7. HeyGen

    AI avatar videos and video translation.

    Verdict: Avatar presenters and lip-synced translation when you don't want to be on camera.

Picks are editorial and not paid. Pricing and features change often — check each official site.
