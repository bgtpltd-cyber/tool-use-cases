Best AI for video and YouTube

Generate footage, edit faster, voice it, and cut it into Shorts.

Last checked: Oct 22, 2026 · Published by Lali Publications. Picks researched by our AI editor and checked against each tool's official site. How we pick

Quick answer: Descript is our top pick. Edit talking-head videos like a doc; removes filler words. Huge time saver for YouTubers.

Our picks

  1. 1. Descript

    Edit video and podcasts by editing the transcript.

    Verdict: Edit talking-head videos like a doc; removes filler words. Huge time saver for YouTubers.

    Price: Free plan. Hobbyist $16/mo billed yearly ($24 monthly); Creator $24/mo billed yearly ($35 monthly).

  2. 2. Runway

    AI video generation and editing suite (Gen-series models).

    Verdict: Pro-grade AI video generation and editing tools for b-roll and effects.

    Price: Free plan for trying the tools. Standard $15/mo ($12/mo billed yearly); Pro $35/mo ($28/mo billed yearly).

  3. 3. Google Veo

    Google DeepMind's text-to-video model with native audio.

    Verdict: High-quality generated clips with sound; strong for cinematic b-roll.

    Price: Video generation isn't on the free Gemini plan. Paid Google AI plans that include it start at $4.99/mo in the US.

  4. 4. ElevenLabs

    Realistic AI voiceover, voice cloning and dubbing.

    Verdict: Best-in-class voiceover and dubbing into other languages.

    Price: Free plan: 10k credits a month. Starter $6/mo; Creator $22/mo.

  5. 5. OpusClip

    Turns long videos into short vertical clips automatically.

    Verdict: Turns a long video or stream into Shorts/TikToks automatically.

    Price: Free trial, no card needed. Starter $15/mo; Pro $29/mo.

  6. 6. CapCut

    Video editor with a free plan; auto-captions and most AI tools need Pro.

    Verdict: The practical everyday editor with a usable free plan; auto-captions now need Pro.

    Price: Free plan with the basics. Auto-captions need a paid Pro plan.

  7. 7. HeyGen

    AI avatar videos and video translation.

    Verdict: Avatar presenters and lip-synced translation when you don't want to be on camera.

    Price: Free plan for trying it out. Creator from $29/mo; Pro from $49/mo.

How to choose

  • Talking-head videos: Descript lets you edit the transcript like a doc. It has a free plan.
  • Generated b-roll: Runway has a free plan to try it. Google Veo needs a paid Google AI plan (from $4.99/mo in the US).
  • Voiceover or dubbing: ElevenLabs (free plan: 10k credits a month).
  • Shorts from long videos: OpusClip (free trial, then from $15/mo).
  • Everyday edits on a $0 budget: CapCut. Auto-captions need Pro.
  • No camera at all: HeyGen avatars (free plan; Creator from $29/mo).

Prices checked Oct 22, 2026 on each tool's official pricing page. They change often, so the official page wins.

Picks are editorial and not paid. Pricing and features change often — check each official site.
