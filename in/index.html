All 30 use cases (English)

  • Best AI for vibe coding — Describe it, run it, tweak it. Tools that let you build software mostly by talking.
  • Best AI coding agents — Agents that take a task, edit multiple files, run commands and come back with a diff.
  • Best AI for fixing broken code and errors — Paste the stack trace, get a fix. Tools that are good at debugging, not just generating.
  • Best AI for game prototyping — Get a playable idea on screen fast — code, art, 3D and music.
  • Best AI for video and YouTube — Generate footage, edit faster, voice it, and cut it into Shorts.
  • Best AI writing assistants — Drafting, editing and rewriting — docs, blog posts, READMEs, emails.
  • Best AI for research — Find sources, read them, and summarize — with citations you can check.
  • Best AI image generation — Thumbnails, concept art, mockups, memes.
  • Best tools for running AI locally — Private, free-to-run AI on your own machine.
  • Best AI for automation workflows — Connect your apps and let AI handle the repetitive steps — triggers, agents, and glue code.
  • Best AI for landing pages — Go from idea to a live, decent-looking landing page in an afternoon.
  • Best AI for UI design — Turn a prompt or sketch into screens, then into something you can build.
  • Best AI voice and text-to-speech — Voiceovers, narration, cloned voices and app speech without a mic.
  • Best AI for music — Songs, soundtracks and background music from a prompt.
  • Best AI for SEO content — Research, outline and write pages that can rank — without publishing AI slop.
  • Best AI for sales emails — Write better cold and follow-up emails — personal, short, and not obviously AI.
  • Best AI for data and spreadsheets — Analyze CSVs, write formulas and make charts by asking in plain English.
  • Best AI for presentations — Go from outline to a decent slide deck fast.
  • Best AI for 3D assets — Generate props, characters and models from text or images.
  • Best AI for meeting notes — Record, transcribe and summarize calls so nobody has to take notes.
  • Best AI for chatbots and RAG — Build an assistant that answers from your own docs — from no-code to custom code.
  • Best hosting for AI-built apps — Where to ship the app your AI tools just built. These are hosting platforms, not AI tools — picked for how well they fit AI-assisted builders.
  • Best AI for building Chrome extensions — Go from 'there should be a button for this' to a working browser extension.
  • Best AI for YouTube thumbnails and packaging — Thumbnails, titles and the hook that gets the click.
  • Best AI agent frameworks — Code frameworks for building your own agents with tools, memory and multi-step workflows.
  • Best AI for logos and branding — Logo ideas, brand marks and a starter brand kit, fast.
  • Best AI for customer support — Answer customers faster with AI agents and AI-drafted replies.
  • Best AI for web scraping — Turn websites into clean data for apps, research and AI pipelines. Respect each site's terms and robots.txt.
  • Best AI for API testing — Write requests, generate tests and debug failing endpoints.
  • Best AI for notes and docs — Write, organize and search your notes and team docs with AI.
