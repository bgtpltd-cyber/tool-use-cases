Our picks

  1. 1. Fireflies.ai

    AI meeting bot that records, transcribes and summarizes Zoom/Meet/Teams calls.

    Verdict: Top pick: joins your calls automatically, with solid summaries, search and CRM integrations.

  2. 2. Otter.ai

    Real-time meeting transcription and AI summaries, with a usable free tier.

    Verdict: Best free tier and live transcription; summaries are a bit more generic.

  3. 3. Grain

    Meeting recorder built around clips and highlights, popular with sales and customer teams.

    Verdict: Best for sales and customer calls where you want to share clips, not just notes.

  4. 4. Notion AI

    AI writing and Q&A built into Notion workspaces.

    Verdict: Built-in meeting notes if your team already lives in Notion — no extra bot needed.

  5. 5. Claude

    Anthropic's AI assistant, strong at long documents, writing and code.

    Verdict: No bot at all: paste a transcript and get sharper summaries and action items. Skip it if you want automatic recording.

Picks are editorial and not paid. Pricing and features change often — check each official site.
