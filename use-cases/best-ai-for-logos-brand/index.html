Our picks

  1. 1. Recraft

    AI image generator for designers that outputs editable vector (SVG) graphics and icons.

    Verdict: Top pick: generates real vector (SVG) logos and icons you can edit and scale.

  2. 2. Ideogram

    Image generator especially good at legible text in images.

    Verdict: Best for logo concepts with readable wordmarks; you'll still need to vectorize the one you pick.

  3. 3. Looka

    AI logo maker that generates a logo plus a brand kit for a one-time fee.

    Verdict: Easiest all-in-one logo plus brand kit for non-designers; results can feel template-y.

  4. 4. Midjourney

    Image generator known for strong aesthetic, artistic output.

    Verdict: Great for exploring brand mood and visual direction rather than final logo files.

  5. 5. ChatGPT Images

    OpenAI image generation inside ChatGPT, good at following instructions and text.

    Verdict: Handy for iterating on a concept by conversation. Check that it's distinctive before trademarking anything.

Picks are editorial and not paid. Pricing and features change often — check each official site.
