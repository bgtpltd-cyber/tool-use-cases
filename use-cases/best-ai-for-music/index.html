Our picks

  1. 1. Suno

    Generates full songs, handy for placeholder game/video music.

    Verdict: Top pick: complete songs with vocals in seconds; easiest to get something good.

  2. 2. Udio

    AI song generator known for high-fidelity vocals and fine-grained remixing.

    Verdict: Strong vocals and finer remix control; worth trying when Suno's output feels generic.

  3. 3. AIVA

    AI composer for instrumental, orchestral and soundtrack music, with MIDI export.

    Verdict: Best for instrumental and orchestral scores with MIDI export you can edit in a DAW.

  4. 4. ElevenLabs

    Realistic AI voiceover, voice cloning and dubbing.

    Verdict: Has music and sound-effect generation alongside voice, handy if you already use it.

Picks are editorial and not paid. Pricing and features change often — check each official site.
