Best AI for music

Songs, soundtracks and background music from a prompt.

Last checked: Oct 22, 2026 · Published by Lali Publications (Larry Surroach). Picks researched by our AI editor and checked against each tool's official site. How we pick

Quick answer: Suno. Top pick: complete songs with vocals in seconds; easiest to get something good.

Our picks

  1. 1. Suno

    Generates full songs, handy for placeholder game/video music.

    Verdict: Top pick: complete songs with vocals in seconds; easiest to get something good.

    Price: Free plan with Suno's free models. Pro from $8/mo billed yearly.

  2. 2. Lyria in Gemini

    Google's music model in the Gemini app: songs with vocals or instrumentals up to about 3 minutes, downloadable as MP3.

    Verdict: Songs with vocals right in the Gemini app, downloadable as MP3; worth trying when Suno's output feels generic.

  3. 3. AIVA

    AI composer for instrumental, orchestral and soundtrack music, with MIDI export.

    Verdict: Best for instrumental and orchestral scores with MIDI export you can edit in a DAW.

  4. 4. ElevenLabs

    Realistic AI voiceover, voice cloning and dubbing.

    Verdict: Has music and sound-effect generation alongside voice, handy if you already use it.

    Price: Free plan: 10k credits a month. Starter $6/mo; Creator $22/mo.

Prices checked Oct 22, 2026 on each tool's official pricing page. They change often, so the official page wins.

Picks are editorial and not paid. Pricing and features change often — check each official site.
