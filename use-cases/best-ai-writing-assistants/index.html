Best AI writing assistants

Drafting, editing and rewriting — docs, blog posts, READMEs, emails.

Last checked: Oct 22, 2026 · Published by Lali Publications. Picks researched by our AI editor and checked against each tool's official site. How we pick

Quick answer: Claude is our top pick. The most natural-sounding prose of the big assistants; great editor.

Our picks

  1. 1. Claude

    Anthropic's AI assistant, strong at long documents, writing and code.

    Verdict: The most natural-sounding prose of the big assistants; great editor.

    Price: Free plan. Pro $20/mo ($17/mo billed yearly); Max $100/mo; Ultra $200/mo.

  2. 2. ChatGPT

    OpenAI's general-purpose AI assistant with chat, images, voice and search.

    Verdict: Versatile all-rounder for drafts, rewrites and variations in one chat.

    Price: Free plan. Paid plans: Go, Plus ($20/mo) and Pro.

  3. 3. Notion AI

    AI writing and Q&A built into Notion workspaces.

    Verdict: Best if your notes and docs already live in Notion.

    Price: Free and Plus ($10 per member/mo billed yearly) include only a trial of Notion AI. Full Notion AI (Notion Agent, AI Meeting Notes) is on Business, $20 per member/mo billed yearly.

  4. 4. Grammarly

    Writing assistant for grammar, tone and rewrites everywhere you type.

    Verdict: Polish anywhere you type — browser, email, docs.

    Price: Free plan for spelling and grammar. Pro from $12/mo adds full-sentence rewrites.

  5. 5. Gemini

    Google's AI assistant with huge context windows and Google integration.

    Verdict: Convenient inside Google Docs and Gmail.

    Price: Free plan with a Google Account (includes Canvas and Deep Research). In the US: Google AI Plus $4.99/mo, Google AI Pro $19.99/mo, Ultra from $99.99/mo.

How to choose

  • You want prose that sounds like a person wrote it, and a strict editor: Claude. The free plan is enough to try it on a real draft and allows up to 5 Projects, so you can keep a style guide and samples in one. Pro is $20/mo, or $17/mo billed yearly.
  • You want many versions fast: headlines, rewrites, tone changes: ChatGPT. Ask for five options at once and keep the best lines. The free plan works for this; Plus ($20/mo) adds more messages and uploads.
  • Your drafts and notes already live in Notion: Notion AI. Free and Plus only include a trial of Notion AI; the full version is on Business at $20 per member/mo billed yearly.
  • You need polish everywhere you type, not a chat window: Grammarly. Free fixes spelling and grammar and includes 100 AI prompts a month. Pro (from $12/mo) adds full-sentence rewrites, tone suggestions and 2,000 prompts per member a month.
  • You write in Gmail and Google Docs all day: Gemini. The Gemini app is free with a Google Account. In the US, Google AI Plus ($4.99/mo) adds Gemini in Gmail; Gemini in Docs is listed from Google AI Pro ($19.99/mo).

Whatever you pick, give it your own outline and a sample of your writing first, and read every line before you send or publish. For cold outreach, see Best AI for sales emails; for pages meant to rank, see Best AI for SEO content.
