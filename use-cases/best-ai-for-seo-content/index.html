Best AI for SEO content

Research, outline and write pages that can rank — without publishing AI slop.

Last checked: Oct 22, 2026 · Published by Lali Publications (Larry Surroach). Picks researched by our AI editor and checked against each tool's official site. How we pick

Quick answer: Claude. Top pick for the actual writing: the most natural drafts and the best editor of your own material.

Our picks

  1. 1. Claude

    Anthropic's AI assistant, strong at long documents, writing and code.

    Verdict: Top pick for the actual writing: the most natural drafts and the best editor of your own material.

    Price: Free plan. Pro $20/mo ($17/mo billed yearly); Max from $100/mo.

  2. 2. Surfer

    SEO and AI-search visibility tool (formerly SurferSEO) with a content editor that scores drafts against top-ranking pages.

    Verdict: Scores your draft against what ranks; useful guardrails, but don't write to the score alone.

  3. 3. Clearscope

    Premium content-optimization tool for keyword coverage and content grading.

    Verdict: Premium content grading for teams and agencies; overkill for a solo site.

  4. 4. Perplexity

    AI answer engine that searches the web and cites sources.

    Verdict: Fast cited research on a topic and on what competitors cover.

  5. 5. ChatGPT

    OpenAI's general-purpose AI assistant with chat, images, voice and search.

    Verdict: Solid all-rounder for briefs, outlines and meta descriptions; deep research for topic gaps.

    Price: Free plan. Paid plans: Go, Plus ($20/mo) and Pro.

Prices checked Oct 22, 2026 on each tool's official pricing page. They change often, so the official page wins.

Picks are editorial and not paid. Pricing and features change often — check each official site.
