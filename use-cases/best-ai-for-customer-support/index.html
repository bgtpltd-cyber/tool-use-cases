Best AI for customer support

Answer customers faster with AI agents and AI-drafted replies.

Last checked: Oct 22, 2026 · Published by Lali Publications. Picks researched by our AI editor and checked against each tool's official site. How we pick

Quick answer: Intercom Fin. Top pick for resolving tickets automatically; works even without the Intercom help desk. Priced per resolution, so costs grow with volume.

Our picks

  1. 1. Intercom Fin

    Intercom's AI support agent that resolves customer questions from your help content.

    Verdict: Top pick for resolving tickets automatically; works even without the Intercom help desk. Priced per resolution, so costs grow with volume.

  2. 2. Zendesk AI

    AI agents and agent-assist features built into the Zendesk help desk.

    Verdict: Natural choice if you already run on Zendesk; heavier setup and pricing for small teams.

  3. 3. Help Scout

    Simple shared inbox and help desk with AI drafts, summaries and AI Answers.

    Verdict: Best for small teams that want a simple inbox with AI drafts and answers, not a big platform.

  4. 4. Claude

    Anthropic's AI assistant, strong at long documents, writing and code.

    Verdict: No platform needed: paste a ticket plus your docs and get a careful, friendly reply draft.

    Price: Free plan. Pro $20/mo ($17/mo billed yearly); Max from $100/mo.

  5. 5. ChatGPT

    OpenAI's general-purpose AI assistant with chat, images, voice and search.

    Verdict: Same idea; keep your docs and tone guide in a ChatGPT Project so every draft starts from the same context.

    Price: Free plan. Paid plans: Go, Plus ($20/mo) and Pro.

Prices checked Oct 22, 2026 on each tool's official pricing page. They change often, so the official page wins.

Picks are editorial and not paid. Pricing and features change often — check each official site.
