Best AI for notes and docs

Write, organize and search your notes and team docs with AI.

Last checked: Oct 22, 2026 · Published by Lali Publications. Picks researched by our AI editor and checked against each tool's official site. How we pick

Quick answer: Notion AI. Top pick if your workspace is in Notion: AI writing, Q&A across pages and meeting notes in one place.

Our picks

  1. 1. Notion AI

    AI writing and Q&A built into Notion workspaces.

    Verdict: Top pick if your workspace is in Notion: AI writing, Q&A across pages and meeting notes in one place.

    Price: Free and Plus ($10 per member/mo billed yearly) include only a trial of Notion AI. Full Notion AI (Notion Agent, AI Meeting Notes) is on Business, $20 per member/mo billed yearly.

  2. 2. Claude

    Anthropic's AI assistant, strong at long documents, writing and code.

    Verdict: Best writer and editor for the docs themselves; Projects keep reference material handy.

    Price: Free plan. Pro $20/mo ($17/mo billed yearly); Max from $100/mo.

  3. 3. ChatGPT

    OpenAI's general-purpose AI assistant with chat, images, voice and search.

    Verdict: Versatile for drafting and restructuring docs, from first outline to full rewrite.

    Price: Free plan. Paid plans: Go, Plus ($20/mo) and Pro.

  4. 4. Superhuman Docs (formerly Coda)

    Doc-meets-spreadsheet workspace (formerly Coda) with tables, automations and built-in AI.

    Verdict: Pick it when your docs are really mini-apps with tables and automations.

  5. 5. Mem

    AI notes app that auto-organizes notes and lets you chat with them.

    Verdict: Personal notes that organize themselves; skip it for team wikis.

    Price: Free plan. Mem Plus $9/mo; Mem Pro $29/mo.

Prices checked Oct 22, 2026 and Oct 23, 2026 on each tool's official pricing page. They change often, so the official page wins.

Picks are editorial and not paid. Pricing and features change often — check each official site.
