Best AI for notes and docs

Write, organize and search your notes and team docs with AI.

Last checked: Oct 22, 2026 · Published by Lali Publications. Picks researched by our AI editor and checked against each tool's official site. How we pick

Quick answer: Notion AI. Top pick if your workspace is in Notion: AI writing, Q&A across pages and meeting notes in one place.

Our picks

  1. 1. Notion AI

    AI writing and Q&A built into Notion workspaces.

    Verdict: Top pick if your workspace is in Notion: AI writing, Q&A across pages and meeting notes in one place.

    Price: Free and Plus ($10 per member/mo billed yearly) include only a trial of Notion AI. Full Notion AI (Notion Agent, AI Meeting Notes) is on Business, $20 per member/mo billed yearly.

  2. 2. Claude

    Anthropic's AI assistant, strong at long documents, writing and code.

    Verdict: Best writer and editor for the docs themselves; Projects keep reference material handy.

    Price: Free plan. Pro $20/mo ($17/mo billed yearly); Max $100/mo; Ultra $200/mo.

  3. 3. ChatGPT

    OpenAI's general-purpose AI assistant with chat, images, voice and search.

    Verdict: Versatile for drafting and restructuring docs, from first outline to full rewrite.

    Price: Free plan. Paid plans: Go, Plus ($20/mo) and Pro.

  4. 4. Microsoft 365 Copilot (OneNote and Word)

    Copilot inside OneNote and Word: summarize and organize notes, and draft or rewrite documents where your Microsoft 365 files already live.

    Verdict: Best if your notes and Word docs already live in Microsoft 365. Copilot in OneNote and Word is included with Personal, Family, Premium and Pro (AI for the subscription owner only), and with business Copilot plans.

    Price: Copilot in OneNote and Word is included with Microsoft 365 Personal ($9.99/mo or $99.99/yr) and Family ($12.99/mo or $129.99/yr; AI for the subscription owner only). Premium ($19.99/mo or $199.99/yr) and Pro ($99.99/mo, monthly billing only) include the same in-app Copilot with higher usage limits. Microsoft 365 Basic does not include AI features. For work, it is included with Business Standard with Copilot ($23.50 per user/mo billed yearly) and Business Premium with Copilot ($32.00 per user/mo billed yearly), or as the Microsoft 365 Copilot Business add-on ($21.00 per user/mo billed yearly).

  5. 5. Superhuman Docs (formerly Coda)

    Doc-meets-spreadsheet workspace (formerly Coda) with tables, automations and built-in AI.

    Verdict: Pick it when your docs are really mini-apps with tables and automations.

Mem is shutting down. Notes go read-only on Dec 18, 2026, and the service closes on Jan 15, 2027. Don't start there; if you already use it, export your notes now.

How to choose

  • Your team's wiki and docs already live in Notion: Notion AI. Free and Plus only include a trial; full Notion AI (Notion Agent, AI Meeting Notes) is on Business at $20 per member/mo billed yearly. Custom Agents are free to try, then $10 per 1,000 Notion credits.
  • Your docs are really trackers, tables and automations: Superhuman Docs (formerly Coda). You pay only for Doc Makers; Editors and Viewers are free. Its Docs AI assistant is included on paid plans while in beta, with limited trials on Free.
  • You need one long doc written or rewritten well: Claude. Put your reference files in a Project so every draft uses them, then paste the result into your wiki.
  • Your notes and Word documents already live in Microsoft 365: Microsoft 365 Copilot (OneNote and Word). Copilot in OneNote and Word is included with Personal ($9.99/mo or $99.99/yr) and Family ($12.99/mo or $129.99/yr; AI for the subscription owner only). Premium ($19.99/mo or $199.99/yr) and Pro ($99.99/mo, monthly billing only) include the same in-app Copilot with higher usage limits. Microsoft 365 Basic does not include AI features. For work, it is on Business Standard with Copilot ($23.50 per user/mo billed yearly), Business Premium with Copilot ($32.00 per user/mo billed yearly), or the Microsoft 365 Copilot Business add-on ($21.00 per user/mo billed yearly).
  • You mostly restructure: outlines, summaries, tables from messy notes: ChatGPT. It works with any tool because you paste in and paste out.
  • You wanted a personal notes app that organizes itself: don't start on Mem. It stopped new paid plans, notes go read-only on Dec 18, 2026 and it shuts down on Jan 15, 2027. If you use it now, export from Settings → Exports before then. If you already work in Microsoft 365, OneNote and Word with Copilot is the closer fit than starting a new notes app.

Pick the tool where your docs already live; moving a team wiki is the expensive part, not the AI. To turn calls into notes, see Best AI for meeting notes.
