Best AI for YouTube thumbnails and packaging

Thumbnails, titles and the hook that gets the click.

Last checked: Oct 22, 2026 · Published by Lali Publications. Picks researched by our AI editor and checked against each tool's official site. How we pick

Quick answer: Ideogram. Top pick for thumbnails with bold, readable text baked into the image.

Our picks

  1. 1. Ideogram

    Image generator especially good at legible text in images.

    Verdict: Top pick for thumbnails with bold, readable text baked into the image.

    Price: Eligible free accounts get weekly credits. Plus $20/mo ($15/mo billed yearly); Pro $60/mo ($42/mo billed yearly).

  2. 2. ChatGPT Images

    OpenAI image generation inside ChatGPT, good at following instructions and text.

    Verdict: Best for edits by conversation ("make the face more surprised") and for brainstorming titles in the same chat.

  3. 3. Canva

    Online design tool with Magic Studio AI features and ready-made templates.

    Verdict: Where you actually lay out the final thumbnail: templates, background removal and Magic tools.

  4. 4. Midjourney

    Image generator known for strong aesthetic, artistic output.

    Verdict: Most striking backgrounds and scenes; add the text afterwards in Canva.

    Price: No free plan: every plan is a subscription. Basic $10/mo ($8/mo billed yearly); Standard $30/mo adds unlimited Relax-mode images.

  5. 5. Claude

    Anthropic's AI assistant, strong at long documents, writing and code.

    Verdict: Brainstorm title and thumbnail-text pairs; it's honest when an idea is clickbait-y.

    Price: Free plan. Pro $20/mo ($17/mo billed yearly); Max from $100/mo.

Prices checked Oct 22, 2026 and Oct 23, 2026 on each tool's official pricing page. They change often, so the official page wins.

Picks are editorial and not paid. Pricing and features change often — check each official site.
